Crash, to scale

One engine unit is one inch, and the player is 70 units tall. Measured that way, Crash's walkable space is about the size of a pro soccer pitch, and a player is a speck in it: you would need about 61 people lying head to foot to span the long side.

How tall it gets

Upper floors sit about 11 ft (136 units, roughly two player heights) above the street outside. The highest standing spot is a rooftop about 40 ft above the street next to it, and 47 ft above the lowest street on the map. The street itself falls about 10 ft from one end of the map to the other.

A standing player's eyes are at 60 units (5′0″). A jump clears 39 units (3′3″), which is why waist-high walls and crates work as vaults.

How this was measured

  • Map extents come from the 224 bot waypoints for mp_crash in ineedbots/cod4x_bot_warfare, recorded in-game as engine coordinates. Waypoints mark where a player can stand, so the wall-to-wall size is a little bigger. Expect another 10–20 ft each way.
  • Player size and speed come from the defaults in the CoD4x server source: hull ±15 × 70 units, eye height 60, jump_height 39, g_speed 190, player_sprintSpeedScale 1.5, player_sprintTime 4.0.
  • One unit = one inch is the working scale IW-engine maps are built to: a 70-unit player reads as 5′10″, and doors and stairs are sized to match. It is a modelling convention. Nothing in the engine enforces it.
  • Crossing times assume a straight line at full speed. Real routes bend around cover. The longest route is measured along the waypoint links, which zig-zag a bit, so treat it as an upper estimate.
  • This is the 2007 original. The 2019 Modern Warfare remake of Crash was rebuilt in a different engine and was not measured.
